Who We Are

Building a Dignified Future
for Every Child

AL Hind Educational and Charitable Trust is a registered non-profit working at the grassroots level to uplift marginalized communities through education, skill development, and social welfare.

"We believe education is the most powerful tool to break the cycle of poverty."

From scholarships to health camps, our programs reach the most vulnerable families across Bihar and beyond.

Know Our Story
What We Do

Our Focus Areas

Comprehensive programs designed to uplift communities at every level.

Education Support

Scholarships, learning materials, school enrollment, and academic mentoring for underprivileged children.

Health & Wellbeing

Free health camps, nutrition awareness, and basic medical support for vulnerable families.

Skill Development

Vocational training and life skills programs to promote self-reliance and employment.

Orphan Support

Food, shelter, education and emotional support for orphaned and abandoned children.

Women Empowerment

Training, awareness and financial literacy programs helping women become self-sufficient.

Community Welfare

Flood relief, food distribution, Ramadan aid and emergency support for communities in need.
